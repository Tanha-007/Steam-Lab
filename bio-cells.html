Cell Division - The Process of Life:
Cells divide to grow, repair damage, and reproduce. This diagram shows Mitosis, the process of dividing one cell into two identical cells.
Mitosis Phases:
1. Prophase: Chromosomes condense, nuclear membrane breaks down
2. Metaphase: Chromosomes align at cell center
3. Anaphase: Sister chromatids separate and move to opposite poles
4. Telophase: Nuclear membranes reform, cytokinesis begins
5. Cytokinesis: Cell completely divides into two daughter cells
