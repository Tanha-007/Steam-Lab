DNA - The Blueprint of Life:
DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) contains all genetic instructions for building and maintaining living organisms. It consists of four nitrogenous bases: Adenine (A), Thymine (T), Guanine (G), and Cytosine (C).
Base Pairing Rules (Chargaff's Rules):
• Adenine (A) always pairs with Thymine (T) - 2 hydrogen bonds
• Guanine (G) always pairs with Cytosine (C) - 3 hydrogen bonds

DNA Replication:
During cell division, DNA replicates to create two identical copies. Each strand serves as a template for a new strand.
